What we give riders

Money you can trace, trip by trip.

Every closed trip writes a payout entry at your campaign’s rate (₹2–₹3/km). Watch it land in your wallet — then collect weekly salary over UPI.

💰

Your earnings

₹2–₹3 per verified kilometre, set by the campaign you join. A 20 km day ≈ ₹40–₹60, straight into your payout ledger.

Week chips (All / This week / Last week, Monday-start) plus a salary-week card show earned vs paid. Small balances roll forward until ₹100.

📅

Weekly UPI salary

Salary week runs Monday–Sunday IST. The operator pays via UPI and marks paid with a transaction reference — you get notified in-app instantly.

Full payment history with UPI refs lives in your wallet. One number = one account forever — reinstall with the same number, history intact.
